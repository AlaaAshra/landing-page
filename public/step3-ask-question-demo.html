Which body cavity contains the heart, and what structures surround it?

The heart is contained in the pericardial cavity, which is located within the mediastinum of the thoracic cavity. 1

The mediastinum lies between the two lungs and contains the heart along with structures such as the thymus gland, trachea, esophagus, aorta, great veins, and nerves. 1

The heart is bathed in pericardial fluid, and the thoracic cavity also contains the right and left lungs in the pleural cavities, surrounded by pleural fluid. 1
